Reaching Our Community

United Ministries of Fulton County is dedicated to serving individuals in need through a threefold program approach. Those three programs are: the food pantry, the outreach program for assistance with utilities or rent, and the Christmas Basket program to provide a well filled basket with holiday foods and other items.

Our Story

United Ministries Of Fulton County, Inc was born from the vision and awareness of three local pastors, Donald Maughan, Josef Palat and Allen Jump, of continued need in the Fulton County Community. Drawing from local participating churches, other members of the community and a fully volunteer staff, the three ministries of action; the food pantry, outreach and Christmas baskets support many families.

Our Mission

The purpose of United Ministries is to give assistance in the form of food or funds to Fulton County residents in need, keeping in mind God's purposes.

Our Community Impact

Food Pantry

We provide a variety of nutritious foods to qualifying families on a monthly basis. The food pantry is open on Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 1:00 pm to 3:00 pm.

In 2025, we served 816 orders representing 2523 individuals.

Qualifying income guidelines are here.

The food pantry is closed when holidays fall on a pantry day. We follow the Community Center schedule.

Outreach

Secure shelter is another need recognized in the Fulton County area. The Outreach program addresses that through working with landlords and utility providers to verify amounts and provide funds to them to secure shelter for residents of Fulton County. Applicants, if approved, can receive assistance once per year.

In 2025, there were 220 families supported through Outreach. 132 were helped with rent and 98 with utilities. 2026 need is ahead of 2025.

Applications for the Outreach program are taken at The Cross Church, 3rd and Main, Monday through Wednesday 9 am - 3 pm.

Christmas Baskets

The Christmas Basket Ministry creates a box of food, much like the Food Pantry orders in greater volume. Also, a ham is provided in each one through a donation from St. Joseph Catholic Church in Rochester. Many volunteers assemble these boxes and deliver them about a week before Christmas.

In 2025, there were 217 families served, representing 662 individuals.
